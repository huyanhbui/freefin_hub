The problems we address

Barriers to proving income

3–6 months of salary records

Salary-based lending guidance lists salary records or an employment contract as income evidence. Freelancers need other documents that link project work, payments and actual income. [1]

Late payments create cash-flow gaps

Work completed ≠ cash available

A Vietnamese marketing freelancer interviewed in 2026 described waiting several days for international payments to reach his domestic bank account. [2]

Payment security is still a concern

68% reported having experienced non-payment

Vietnamese freelancers surveyed by PayPal reported not receiving payment for completed work. Clear agreements and payment records matter. [3]

Three core features

Connect users and support transactions

FreeFin connects clients with freelancers. AI helps with messages and documents, and tracks project progress and payment milestones.

Escrow-style payments: the client’s money is held and released to the freelancer when the client accepts the work, or under the agreed terms.

Loan applications and credit guarantees

AI gathers income evidence into one loan application. The platform assesses guarantee eligibility and coverage.

Eligible users may receive a conditional platform guarantee. Users who do not qualify still receive a standard application package. The bank assesses each application and makes the lending decision.

Repayment advances

When a verified, eligible client payment is late, FreeFin may advance funds within agreed limits to cover the bank repayment that is due.

The advance is recovered when the client pays. Costs and repayment terms are disclosed upfront.

How it works

  1. Register

    Verify your identity, create a profile and choose what data to share.

  2. Agree on terms

    Confirm the work, price, deadlines, acceptance rules and payment milestones.

  3. Deliver the work

    Submit the work and record the client’s acceptance.

  4. Receive payment

    The payment is matched to the project. Your income history grows over time.

  5. Request financing support

    AI compiles income evidence. The platform verifies records and assesses guarantee eligibility.

  6. The bank assesses and decides

    The bank makes its own assessment and lends only if it approves.

Value for each participant

Expected value. How FreeFin differs from other options are hypotheses to validate.

Freelancers

  • Verified clients and clear payment milestones
  • Verified income records to support bank loan applications
  • Temporary advances when a verified client payment is late

Clients and businesses

  • Escrow-style payment holds expected to reduce cancelled or undelivered jobs
  • Simpler tracking, work acceptance and invoicing

Banks and lenders

  • A new borrower segment backed by verified data
  • A partial platform credit guarantee expected to reduce default exposure
  • Verified transaction and income history to simplify credit assessment

FreeFin

  • Transaction fees and potential guarantee service margins
  • Network effects between freelancers, clients and financial partners
